RealReady Support

Help with the RealReady mobile app for iPhone, iPad, and Android

Stuck, spotted a wrong answer, or something isn’t working? We read every message and reply personally.

Email support

support@nextchapterapps.com

Include your device (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8), the state you’re studying for, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help.

Common questions

The FAQ covers pricing, which states are included, and how the free tier works. If your question isn’t there, email us.

Restore a purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID or Google account. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, sign in to your RealReady account and open Profile → Restore Purchases. If the store has nothing to restore, the app will say so; if a connection problem gets in the way, it will tell you that instead.

Report a question or explanation

Every question has thumbs up / thumbs down under its explanation. Tap thumbs down and tell us what’s wrong — the report reaches us with the question attached. You can also use Profile → Send feedback.

Study reminders

If you stop studying, the app can send a few reminders over two weeks — never daily. Turn them on or off at Profile → Study reminders, or in your device’s notification settings.

Delete your account

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. This removes your login, profile, quiz history, and feedback from our servers. If you can’t get into the app, email support@nextchapterapps.com from the address on the account and we’ll delete it for you within 30 days.
