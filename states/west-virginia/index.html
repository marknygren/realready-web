Q1 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

A candidate is being considered for appointment to the WVREC. How many of the five commissioners must hold a license under the Real Estate License Act?

  1. A Two; the other three are unlicensed citizen members
  2. B All five must be licensed brokers
  3. C Four; the fifth is an unlicensed citizen member
  4. D Three; the other two are unlicensed citizen members
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: C Four; the fifth is an unlicensed citizen member

W. Va. Code §30-40-6(b)(1) requires four of the five commissioners to be licensed under the article — as a broker, associate broker OR salesperson — and one to be a citizen member who is not licensed. Each licensed commissioner must have been licensed and practiced in West Virginia as their primary vocation for at least 10 years immediately preceding appointment, and every commissioner must have been a state resident for at least six years.

Q2 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

What type of accounts is the WVREC authorized to audit?

  1. A Broker trust accounts and business records
  2. B Title company escrow accounts
  3. C Salesperson personal bank accounts
  4. D Client mortgage loan accounts
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: A Broker trust accounts and business records

The WVREC may perform compliance audits on brokerage offices (§30-40-7(q)) and must be authorized by every broker to make periodic inspections of trust fund accounts (§30-40-18(j)). Records must be open to inspection at the broker's usual place of business during regular business hours. Title company escrow accounts are outside the Commission's jurisdiction.

Q3 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

What types of administrative sanctions can the WVREC impose on a licensee?

  1. A Fines, suspension, or revocation
  2. B Only written warnings for first offenses
  3. C Only fines, no license action
  4. D Only license revocation, no fines
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: A Fines, suspension, or revocation

Under §30-40-7(l) the WVREC "may impose one or more sanctions as considered appropriate," and the list is broader than fines and license action: denial of a license or renewal, an administrative fine not to exceed $1,000 per day per violation, probation, revocation, suspension, restitution, additional education, censure, denial of future license, downgrade of license, and reprimand.

Q5 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

Which WV license type allows the holder to operate independently and supervise salespersons?

  1. A Broker
  2. B Managing agent
  3. C Salesperson
  4. D Associate broker
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: A Broker

Only a broker can operate independently and supervise salespersons in WV. An associate broker holds broker qualifications but works under another broker, similar to a salesperson.

Q6 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

By what date must a WV real estate license be renewed each year to avoid expiring?

  1. A June 30
  2. B January 31
  3. C July 1
  4. D December 31
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: A June 30

174-1-8.1: "All licenses expire on June 30 regardless of the date the license was issued." The license term coincides with the fiscal year (§30-40-17(c)(5)). The WVREC opens its online renewal portal on May 1, but June 30 is the operative deadline; after that the license is expired and late fees apply through December 31.

Q8 of 20 State Practice

A WV salesperson is asked to prepare a deed for a client. Can the salesperson legally do this?

  1. A No, that constitutes unauthorized practice of law
  2. B Yes, with a disclaimer that it is not legal advice
  3. C Yes, if the broker approves
  4. D Yes, for standard residential transactions
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: A No, that constitutes unauthorized practice of law

Licensees may fill in blanks on approved forms but may not draft legal documents or give legal advice. Preparing a deed is the practice of law and requires an attorney.

Q9 of 20 State Practice

Under 174-1-17.1, which set of items must appear in a WV licensee's advertisement of a property?

  1. A The property's tax parcel number and assessed value
  2. B The licensee's name and WVREC license number
  3. C The responsible broker's name and nothing more
  4. D Company name, responsible broker's name with "Broker," office address, and a phone number
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: D Company name, responsible broker's name with "Broker," office address, and a phone number

174-1-17.1 requires each advertisement of property or a specific service to include four things: the company name, the name of the responsible broker together with the term "Broker," the physical address of a licensed office location, and a phone number. Those first three items must be displayed at no less than half the size of any associate broker, salesperson or team name in the ad. Yard signs are excused from the physical address requirement.

Q10 of 20 State Practice

At what type of financial institution must a WV broker maintain a trust fund account?

  1. A A state-chartered commercial bank only
  2. B Any financial institution located in West Virginia
  3. C An institution specifically chartered by the WVREC
  4. D One insured against loss by a federal agency
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: D One insured against loss by a federal agency

§30-40-18(c): each trust fund account "shall be established at a financial institution which is insured against loss by an agency of the federal government and the amount deposited therein cannot exceed the amount that is insured against loss." The test is federal insurance, not FDIC specifically — an NCUA-insured credit union qualifies too.

Q12 of 20 State Practice

Which agency type in WV represents both the buyer and seller in the same transaction?

  1. A Seller's agent
  2. B Buyer's agent
  3. C Dual agent
  4. D Transaction coordinator
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: C Dual agent

Representing both sides is dual agency, and §30-40-19(a)(7) plus 174-1-16.2 require the written consent of both buyer and seller first. West Virginia's promulgated Notice of Agency form uses two specific labels for it: "Limited Dual Agent" where one licensee represents both parties, and "Designated Dual Agent" where the brokerage assigns a separate licensee to each side.

Q13 of 20 State Transactions

Which protected class is unique to West Virginia's Fair Housing Act and listed separately from general disability?

  1. A Sexual orientation
  2. B Age
  3. C Blindness
  4. D Marital status
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: C Blindness

§16B-18-5 lists blindness as its own protected characteristic alongside disability, so West Virginia protects blind applicants explicitly rather than folding them into a general disability category. Age, marital status and sexual orientation are not protected characteristics under the West Virginia Fair Housing Act.

Q14 of 20 State Transactions

Under WV's Fair Housing Act, the Mrs. Murphy exemption applies to owner-occupied buildings with how many or fewer units?

  1. A Three units
  2. B Two units
  3. C Six units
  4. D Four units
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: D Four units

§16B-18-4(a)(2) covers "rooms or units in dwellings containing living quarters occupied or intended to be occupied by no more than four families living independently of each other, if the owner actually maintains and occupies one of such living quarters as his residence." Note the critical West Virginia limit: §16B-18-4(a) makes this exemption available "solely for the purposes of familial status," so it is no defence to a claim based on race, colour, religion, ancestry, sex, blindness, disability or national origin.

Q17 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

The WVREC finds a salesperson guilty of commingling trust funds. Which sanction can the commission impose?

  1. A Automatic permanent license revocation
  2. B Administrative fine and license suspension
  3. C Civil monetary damages to the client
  4. D Criminal imprisonment only
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: B Administrative fine and license suspension

The WVREC can fine (up to $1,000 per day per violation), suspend, revoke, downgrade, or place a licensee on probation, and may order restitution or the return of compensation collected from an injured consumer (§30-40-7(l)). It does not imprison — criminal charges are brought by the county prosecuting attorney on referral (§30-40-24(b)) — and it does not enter civil judgments for damages, though commingling is separately a disciplinary ground under §30-40-19(a)(9).

Q18 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

How does an associate broker differ from a salesperson in West Virginia?

  1. A Associate broker supervises salespersons
  2. B Associate broker has broker qualifications
  3. C Associate broker can operate independently
  4. D There is no meaningful difference
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: B Associate broker has broker qualifications

An associate broker holds broker qualifications (education and experience) but chooses to work under another broker rather than operating independently. A salesperson has only salesperson-level qualifications.

Q19 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

In West Virginia, what is the difference between an 'expired' license and a 'cancelled' license?

  1. A Both terms mean the same license status
  2. B Expired means not renewed by July 1; cancelled means still not renewed by December 31
  3. C Cancelled means not renewed by July 1; expired means still not renewed by December 31
  4. D Cancelled is voluntary; expired is involuntary
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: B Expired means not renewed by July 1; cancelled means still not renewed by December 31

Both are defined in §30-40-4. "Expired" means a license that was not renewed by July 1 — the day after the June 30 deadline. "Cancelled" means a license that was not renewed by December 31 of the year in which the license expired. So expiration comes first and can still be cured with a late fee; cancellation is the further lapse and requires reapplying and re-examining.

Q20 of 20 Licensing & Regulation

An unlicensed person collects rent on several apartment buildings owned by different, unrelated owners, charging each owner a monthly fee. Does this require a WV real estate license?

  1. A Only if the person also manages repairs
  2. B No, rent collection never requires a license
  3. C Yes, collecting rent for compensation requires a license
  4. D Only if the building has 10+ units
Show answer & explanation +

Correct: C Yes, collecting rent for compensation requires a license

Collecting rent for compensation falls within the §30-40-4 definition of a broker, and "property management" is itself a defined licensed activity. Watch the boundary: §30-40-5(c)(8) exempts a person "employed exclusively to act as the management or rental agent for the real estate of one person or entity," and (c)(2) exempts an owner's regular employees who perform incidental acts without extra compensation. Serving several unrelated owners for a fee falls outside both exemptions.

About the exam

More about the West Virginia real estate exam

West Virginia scores its national and state portions separately, so a strong national score won't rescue a weak state score. West Virginia layers its own license law on top of the national principles, and that West Virginia-specific material is exactly where unprepared candidates lose points.

West Virginia tests the duties and powers of the Real Estate Commission, the statutes that govern licensee conduct, the state's agency-disclosure rules, trust-account requirements, and West Virginia's own fair housing protections. FAQ

West Virginia real estate exam: your questions answered

How many questions are on the West Virginia real estate exam?

The West Virginia salesperson exam has 130 multiple-choice questions, split into an 80-question national portion and a 50-question state portion. You get 210 minutes in total, and you need 70% on each portion to pass.

What's the passing score for the West Virginia real estate exam?

You need 70% on each portion separately. The national and state sections are scored independently, so you have to clear both. You receive a score report marked pass or fail at the test center.

How much does the West Virginia real estate exam cost?

The examination fee is $59 per exam part, paid to Pearson VUE when you reserve your seat. Because the salesperson exam has a national part and a state part, plan for that fee on each part you need to take.

How long is the West Virginia real estate exam?

You get 150 minutes for the 80-question national portion and 60 minutes for the 50-question state portion, 210 minutes in all. That is a little over a minute per question, so a steady pace matters.

Who administers the West Virginia real estate exam?

The West Virginia Real Estate Salesperson Exam is administered by Pearson VUE for the West Virginia Real Estate Commission. You register and schedule your exam date through Pearson VUE.

Is the West Virginia real estate exam hard?

It is challenging, especially the state portion. Many candidates handle the national real estate principles fine but underestimate how much West Virginia-specific law the state section covers across its 50 questions. Focused state prep is what separates first-time passers from retakes.

What's on the West Virginia real estate salesperson exam?

The national portion covers property ownership, land use controls, valuation, financing, agency, property disclosures, contracts, leasing and property management, transfer of title, the practice of real estate, and real estate calculations. The state portion focuses on the duties and powers of the West Virginia Real Estate Commission, the statutes that govern licensee activity, agency-disclosure rules, broker trust-account requirements, and West Virginia fair housing law.

What's the best way to prepare for the West Virginia real estate exam?

Practice with West Virginia-specific questions until you score above 80% consistently, then keep drilling the topics you miss most. Review every explanation, not just the ones you get wrong, and use practice tests that mirror the real exam's split national-and-state format and difficulty.
